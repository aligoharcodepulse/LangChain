On the field where dreams take flight,
Under the sun or stadium light,
The bat goes high, the ball goes far,
Cricket shines like a shining star.

Eleven players stand as one,
Chasing wickets, runs, and fun,
The bowler runs with all his might,
The batsman waits for the perfect strike.

Six balls make an over complete,
With every delivery comes a heartbeat,
A four rolls fast across the ground,
A six sends cheers all around.

The keeper stands behind the wicket,
Waiting patiently to grab a ticket,
The fielders run both left and right,
Trying to make the perfect flight.

Some play Tests that last for days,
Some play T20s with faster pace,
ODIs bring a balanced game,
But every format earns its fame.

A captain leads with skill and pride,
Making clever moves from side to side,
With teamwork, courage, and belief,
Every player hopes to bring victory and relief.

From village grounds to stadiums grand,
Cricket is loved across the land,
With every run and every cheer,
It brings the game and people near.

So raise the bat and hear the crowd,
Play with courage, strong and proud,
For cricket is more than bat and ball,
It's passion, teamwork, and dreams for all.
